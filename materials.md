# The World-Builder's Material Encyclopedia
## Autonomous Agent Reference Guide

Welcome, Agent. This database contains records of the elemental and mineral resources you may encounter in this sealed environment. Use this knowledge to identify raw materials, understand their properties, and craft the infrastructure of your world.

---

### Aluminum (from Bauxite)
*   **Identification:** Bauxite ore is reddish-brown, white, or tan and looks like clay/soil. Pure aluminum is a silver-white, lightweight metal.
*   **Properties:** Very light (less than 3x dense as water), ductile, strong, and corrosion-resistant.
*   **Crafting Uses:** Transportation vehicles, building construction, packaging, and electrical applications.

### Antimony (from Stibnite)
*   **Identification:** Silvery-gray, brittle semi-metal. Often found as the mineral stibnite.
*   **Properties:** Expands when it cools/freezes. Poor conductor. 
*   **Crafting Uses:** Flame retardants, hardening lead for batteries, semiconductors, and low-friction machine bearings.

### Barite
*   **Identification:** Usually colorless or milky white, relatively soft (3-3.5 hardness). Unusually heavy for a non-metallic mineral.
*   **Properties:** High density, chemically inert, insoluble, strongly absorbs X-rays and gamma rays.
*   **Crafting Uses:** Weighting agent in drilling, radiation shielding, brake pads and clutches, and heavy additive in paints/plastics.

### Beryllium (from Beryl)
*   **Identification:** Hard, silvery-white metal. Very light weight.
*   **Properties:** High melting point (2349°F), highly elastic, and absorbs neutrons. **Warning:** Beryllium dust is toxic.
*   **Crafting Uses:** Aerospace alloys, elastic springs/gears, non-sparking tools, and nuclear reaction control rods.

### Coal
*   **Identification:** Black or brownish-black combustible sedimentary rock. Varieties include Lignite, Subbituminous, Bituminous, and Anthracite (hardest).
*   **Properties:** High energy content; releases heat when burned.
*   **Crafting Uses:** Primary fuel source for space heating, forging, and generating electricity. Used to make coke for steel production.

### Cobalt
*   **Identification:** Bluish-gray, shiny, brittle metallic element.
*   **Properties:** Naturally magnetic, highly resistant to heat and wear.
*   **Crafting Uses:** Creating deep blue glass/ceramics, strong magnets, and superalloys for high-temperature engines.

### Copper
*   **Identification:** Reddish-orange metal, often tarnishes to green (patina).
*   **Properties:** Highly malleable, ductile, and an excellent conductor of electricity and heat.
*   **Crafting Uses:** Electrical wiring, power transmission, plumbing pipes, and roofing. Can be alloyed with tin to make Bronze, or with zinc to make Brass.

### Gypsum
*   **Identification:** Very soft (can be scratched by a fingernail), white/gray/colorless sedimentary rock or crystal.
*   **Properties:** Contains water in its crystal structure. When heated (calcined), it forms Plaster of Paris.
*   **Crafting Uses:** Interior wallboard, plaster products, and as an additive to Portland cement.

### Halite (Salt)
*   **Identification:** Colorless or white cubic crystals. Dissolves easily in water.
*   **Properties:** Soluble, acts as a preservative and a melting agent for ice.
*   **Crafting Uses:** Food preservation, chemical production (chlorine, caustic soda), water softening, and de-icing.

### Iron Ore (Hematite & Magnetite)
*   **Identification:** Dark, silvery-gray metal. Hematite is red/brown, Magnetite is black. 
*   **Properties:** Magnetic, very reactive, oxidizes (rusts) easily. Very strong when alloyed.
*   **Crafting Uses:** The foundation of modern construction. Alloyed with carbon and other metals to make Steel for buildings, tools, machinery, and vehicles.

### Kaolin (Clay)
*   **Identification:** Earthy, fine-grained material.
*   **Properties:** Plastic and moldable when wet. Becomes rock-hard when fired at high temperatures.
*   **Crafting Uses:** Pottery, bricks, cement, high-quality paper, and heat-resistant (refractory) products.

### Lead (from Galena)
*   **Identification:** Blue-gray, very heavy, metallic element.
*   **Properties:** Extremely soft, heavy, absorbs radiation. **Warning:** Toxic to biological organisms.
*   **Crafting Uses:** Batteries, radiation shielding, weights, ballast, and protective coatings.

### Limestone
*   **Identification:** Commonly a white to gray sedimentary rock, sometimes containing fossils.
*   **Properties:** Contains calcium carbonate. Can be burned to produce quicklime.
*   **Crafting Uses:** Creating cement, mortar, plaster, and as a flux in steel manufacturing.

### Lithium
*   **Identification:** Silvery-white metal.
*   **Properties:** The lightest metal. Highly reactive with oxygen and water (bounces on water). Must be stored in oil.
*   **Crafting Uses:** High-capacity batteries, glass, ceramics, and strong lightweight alloys for aerospace.

### Manganese
*   **Identification:** Gray-white metal with a pinkish tinge. Hard and brittle.
*   **Properties:** Hardens other metals when alloyed. 
*   **Crafting Uses:** Essential for steel production. Used to make construction I-beams, machinery, and dry cell batteries.

### Molybdenum
*   **Identification:** Metallic, silvery-white.
*   **Properties:** Extremely high melting point (4730°F), chemically stable. Soft on its own but adds extreme strength to alloys.
*   **Crafting Uses:** High-strength and stainless steel alloys, rifle barrels, light bulb filaments, and extreme-temperature dry lubricants.

### Nickel
*   **Identification:** Silvery, shiny, metallic element.
*   **Properties:** Malleable, ferro-magnetic (slightly), highly resistant to corrosion and extreme temperatures.
*   **Crafting Uses:** Stainless steel production, rechargeable batteries, and anti-corrosion plating.

### Quartz (Silicon / Silica)
*   **Identification:** Hard (7), glassy luster, often forms hexagonal crystals. Found abundantly in sand.
*   **Properties:** Semiconductor (conducts electricity, but selectively). Very hard and durable.
*   **Crafting Uses:** Glassmaking, ceramics, semiconductor chips (computers), solar cells, and abrasive tools.

### Rare Earth Elements (e.g., Neodymium, Cerium)
*   **Identification:** A set of 17 nearly indistinguishable lustrous silvery-white soft heavy metals.
*   **Properties:** Found in compounds, difficult to isolate. Possess unique magnetic, luminescent, and electrochemical properties.
*   **Crafting Uses:** Permanent magnets for electronics, wind turbines, rechargeable batteries, lasers, and optical glass.

### Silver
*   **Identification:** Bright, white metallic luster.
*   **Properties:** The best reflector of light. Best electrical conductor of all metals. Tarnishes in air. Malleable and ductile.
*   **Crafting Uses:** High-end electronics, electrical contacts, mirrors, photography, and jewelry. 

### Sulfur
*   **Identification:** Bright, lemon-yellow non-metallic element.
*   **Properties:** Very soft (2), low thermal conductivity. Melts easily and burns with a blue flame. Odor of rotten eggs when combined with oxygen.
*   **Crafting Uses:** Sulfuric acid production, fertilizers, rubber vulcanization, and explosives.

### Tantalum
*   **Identification:** Hard, grayish-blue metal.
*   **Properties:** Remarkable resistance to corrosion. Very high melting point (2996°C).
*   **Crafting Uses:** Electronic capacitors, medical implants (bio-compatible), and high-temperature superalloys for jet engines.

### Titanium
*   **Identification:** Silvery-gray metal.
*   **Properties:** High melting temperature, lightweight, highly resistant to corrosion. Strength similar to steel but 45% lighter. Will burn in pure nitrogen.
*   **Crafting Uses:** Aerospace engines and structures, ship parts, white paint pigment (Titanium Dioxide), and surgical implants.

### Vanadium
*   **Identification:** Soft, silver-gray metal.
*   **Properties:** Dramatically hardens and strengthens other metals when alloyed. 
*   **Crafting Uses:** High-strength steel (armor plating, engine parts, skyscrapers) and superconductive magnets.

### Zinc
*   **Identification:** Blue-gray metallic element.
*   **Properties:** Brittle at room temperature but malleable at 100°C. Resists corrosion.
*   **Crafting Uses:** Galvanizing (protecting iron/steel from rust), brass production (when mixed with copper), batteries, and coins.
